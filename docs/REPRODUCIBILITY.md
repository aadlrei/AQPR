# Reproducibility protocol

1. Configure `AQPR_PROJECT_ROOT` and `AQPR_DATA_ROOT`.
2. Obtain datasets under their original access conditions.
3. Use the core notebook sequence documented in the repository README.
4. Skip Notebooks 10 and 11 for article reproduction; they are exploratory context only.
5. Use Notebook 16 and the distributed tables for manuscript-output regeneration.

The curated notebook copies were not re-executed. Reproducibility claims must distinguish preserved original results, mechanically curated code, results regenerated from distributed tables, and full model retraining.
